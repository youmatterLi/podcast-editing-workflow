# Example: one cut, from proposal to check record

All people, dialogue and times below are fictional and only illustrate the method. The host is "Host" and the guest is "Guest".

## 1. Original (fictional source times)

```
[12:03.2] Host: Why did you decide to change jobs back then?
[12:05.8] Guest: Actually, at first I didn't plan to. I was just helping out at a bakery on weekends,
[12:10.4] Guest: then the owner said they were short-staffed, and I, I thought I'd give it a try.
[12:14.9] Guest: But I didn't tell my family.
[12:17.1] Host: And then what?
[12:18.0] Guest: Then they found out, and they were pretty against it. But by then I loved that feeling of firing up the oven before dawn.
```

## 2. Proposed cut (first draft)

To tighten the passage, the proposal deletes 12:05.8–12:14.9 ("Actually, at first … give it a try."), joining it as:

```
Host: Why did you decide to change jobs back then? | Guest: But I didn't tell my family.
```

A separate tightening note, for the case where this passage is kept: remove the stutter "I, I" around 12:13.

## 3. Why it can't be cut that way

- **Question and answer come apart:** the question is "why change jobs", and after the cut the answer contains no reason at all — only "didn't tell my family".
- **Dangling connective:** "But" originally contrasted with "give it a try"; with the premise gone, "But" has nothing to refer to.
- **Creates a meaning the interview didn't have:** the joined version sounds like "the reason for changing jobs was hiding it from family" — a cause the original never stated.
- **Cut-point problem:** the first draft placed the end of the deleted range at the word-timing end of "try", but by level the tail of "try" hasn't decayed to the noise floor yet, so the tail of the deleted word would remain in the output, right before "But".

Fix: keep the reasons (don't delete 12:05.8–12:14.9); remove only the extra "I" in the stutter "I, I", which is a connected-speech micro-cut; if more tightening is needed, delete a whole sentence elsewhere rather than the answer in this question–answer pair.

## 4. Check record (second draft, version v2)

| Item | Status | Evidence | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semantics at the edit (Q&A, references, meaning) | PASS | Read-through of v2 transcript 12:03–12:22 | The answer keeps its reasons; the premise for "But" is still there |
| Micro-cut "I, I" | REVIEW | Re-transcription of the join, level plot | Technical checks show no click or level jump; not listened to by ear, pending the approver listening to this cut |
| Ordinary cut points | N/A | — | No ordinary cuts in this passage in the second draft |
| Whole-piece reference scan | NOT CHECKED | — | Only this passage was checked; the whole-piece reference scan hasn't been run |

Conclusion: v2 is a "review candidate" (it has a NOT CHECKED and an unresolved REVIEW), and must not be described as "checks complete".
